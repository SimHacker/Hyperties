## What the source says

The [introduction](../corpus/personal-history/family/introduc.fil) describes a family-history hypertext
centered on Denis Andrea O'Connell and Paul Edmond O'Connell. It brings together
the O'Connell and Interdonato families through the marriage of Edmond Brendan
O'Connell and Theresa Amelia Interdonato.

It combines oral history, written records, biographies, geographic articles and
family stories, with Irish and Italian ancestry and migration to the United
States. The introduction describes contemporaries "as they are in 1990" and
intends to pass family lore to descendants. It explicitly preserves the
storytellers' differing perspectives rather than homogenizing them. An ancient
ancestry claim in the introduction is a source claim, not independently verified
genealogy.

## Formats and restoration value

- Loose `.FIL` articles: the shared six-offset binary article reader applies.
- Classic `~label~` links and style commands preserve hypertext navigation.
- `index.tie` and `refer.tie` retain index/reference evidence; they should be
  interpreted independently rather than assuming identical schemas.
- `family.bat` preserves launch assumptions. Its duplicate browser was removed
  during approved deduplication; this is not a standalone DOS distribution.
- Browsing `.log` files remain local and Git-ignored. They are operational
  residue, not necessary to present the authored family history.

This is an example of personal knowledge, memory and oral history organized
through hypertext, distinct from both the museum encyclopedia and the book.
The existing non-family semantic catalog and book/museum reading room do not
automatically include it; their reported counts remain non-family counts.

## Publication decision, October 11, 2026

The repository owner requested inclusion of the authored family source and its
README/GLANCE/INDEX documentation so Ben can inspect it before wider sharing,
with redactions and a Git-history rewrite if requested later. This supersedes
the earlier whole-directory exclusion recommendation. No approval from the
family or creator has been established, and Ben's review is not presumed to
substitute for their consent.

The material includes exact life-event dates, relationships and personal
narratives about people who may still be living. These are not repeated in this
analysis, but remain in the included source files. An unannounced public
repository is still public: cloning, indexing and copying can happen immediately.
Deleting files and rewriting Git history later cannot recall outside copies.
Repository visibility is a separate owner-controlled decision.
