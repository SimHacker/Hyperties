# Later Jewish Heritage and Holocaust database

Later museum packed records and companion stores, still images, FLI animation/media actions and browser 3.0.5.100.

## Structure and open questions

The main store is `ENC.HDT`, with seven companions: HFF, HIN, HLN, HNC, HOL, HSD and HUC. Article boundaries, packing and numeric-ID/index mapping remain unresolved; the article and personal-name counts are not established. Forty-two PCX images, seven GIFs, six FLI animations and three PCC files preserve media and interface evidence. Standard image decoding is distinct from recovering proprietary record/index schemas.

See the [museum structure, names and lookup survey](../../../analysis/museum-structure.md#later-edition-and-formats-still-to-recover) and [questions for Ben](../../../analysis/museum-structure.md#questions-for-ben). We need authoring exports or schema/source notes, clarification of PCC and graphic regions, external video/media sources, and evidence of the intended physical installation. Do not assume this is identical to the earlier collection or a victim-name registry.
