Subject groups are entry points, not exclusive classifications. A computing-literature collection can also be a classic packed database and a codec fixture. Those overlapping meanings live in catalog views, not duplicated folders.

Original collection names, internal paths and source bytes are preserved. Modern README/GLANCE/INDEX files are navigation overlays. Historical executables stay with the collection that supplied the retained copy; their presence is evidence, not a runnable-distribution promise.
