                Hypertext is freedom!  As a hypertext user, you
                are free to be creative.  You can uncover
                relationships that were not noticed by anyone
                else.  You can read about things you didn't
                anticipate finding.  The joy of serendipity in
                reading about something novel and the opportunity
                for incidental learning while trying to locate
                a specific fact make hypertext a satisfying 
                adventure.
